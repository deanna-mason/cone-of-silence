Hi everyone — here's Cone of Silence, the project I'm showing today: an
end-to-end encrypted video call room with a podcast recording mode, styled
as a 1960s spy dossier. Calls are peer-to-peer and E2EE; podcast takes are
recorded locally on each host's machine and developed through a real ffmpeg
chain afterward.

Live at https://www.coneofsilence.app — feel free to poke around the lobby
during the demo. Creating a room needs an invite, so if you'd like to try a
call afterward, ping me and I'll mint you one.
